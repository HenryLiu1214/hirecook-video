The core idea
B=f(P×E)
P · Personality Inferred from behavior We don't ask. We watch the candidate act under simulated pressure.
E · Environment Modeled, not assumed Team structure, collaboration density, stress rhythm, decision mode.
B · Behavior The predicted output How they will actually work — in this role, in this team, today.
